# Security & sovereignty

Libra OS is built for organizations that cannot send their data to a vendor.

## Data stays with you

We deploy into your environment and never touch your data. Libra OS runs on your
hardware; with the network cable unplugged it keeps working on an offline license,
with no phone-home.

## The AI firewall

A three-tier screening layer sits in front of the models. It governs what goes in
and what comes out, so answers stay inside policy.

## Grounded and auditable

- Every answer **cites its source** from the open record.
- Routine work runs autonomously; a hard call **freezes and escalates** to a human.
- Every action is logged for audit.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for security bugs. Follow the
[security policy](https://github.com/libraos/community/security/policy) for private
disclosure, or email `contact@meganova.ai`.
